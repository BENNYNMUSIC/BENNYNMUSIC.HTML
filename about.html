About Us

Benny n Music World is an internet radio station streaming 24/7 with a mix of Afrobeat, House, Gospel, and Hip-Hop. We are passionate about bringing fresh music and community content to listeners in South Africa and beyond.

  • Devices: Mobile (70%), Desktop (20%), Smart speakers (10%)
  • Engagement: Average listening time 30 minutes per session
